Use your usual handwriting, one example per box. Six sheets capture one example of every lowercase letter a–z. Repeat the set for more variations; positions change across three sets. Start capture continues after your latest saved sheet.

For f, g, j, p, q and y, put the body on the baseline and any tail below it. These letters keep the drawn baseline; other letters settle onto it automatically. Review samples lets you adjust placement or exclude a sample without deleting it.

On symbol sheets, pale a letters show the context of the blue positioning example. Draw only the labelled symbol in your own handwriting. Symbol placement hides or shows these references and their hints; they are never part of your saved writing. Quotes sit high, commas dip below the baseline, and an underscore belongs just below it.
