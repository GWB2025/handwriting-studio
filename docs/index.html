Practice · not saved

Try one letter in each labelled box. Keep a, c and e small; b and d reach the tall-letter line. Start capture when comfortable.

Use your usual handwriting, one example per box. Choose lowercase, uppercase, numbers, punctuation and symbols, or joined pairs. Each set covers its characters once, in shuffled positions. Lowercase has six sheets. Repeat sets under the same writer name for more samples; each type keeps separate progress. Start capture continues after your latest saved sheet.

For f, g, j, p, q and y, put the body on the baseline and any tail below it. These lowercase letters keep the drawn baseline; other lowercase letters settle onto it automatically. Capitals reach the tall-letter line. Capitals, numbers, punctuation and joined pairs keep their drawn baseline; write joined pairs as a connected shape. Review samples lets you adjust placement or exclude a sample without deleting it.

Practice is optional and is not saved. Switch each guide on or off below. For a baseline only, leave Baseline ticked and untick the other guides and Shading. Letter labels and box dividers stay visible. Open free writing. Pencil or mouse draws; fingers use controls. Save sheets before leaving or refreshing: unsaved drafts are not restored. After capture, use Review samples to check originals, Blend a character to mix two or four examples, and My alphabet to choose preferred versions for Compose.
