Help guide

Ready for Pencil

Use Writing focus for a larger sheet. Show controls brings back the toolbar. Undo, Redo and Save stay within reach. Finish the Pencil stroke before changing the layout.

Use your usual handwriting, one example per box. Choose lowercase, uppercase, numbers, punctuation and symbols, or experimental joined pairs. Each set covers its characters once, in shuffled positions. Lowercase has six sheets. Repeat sets under the same writer name for more samples; each type keeps separate progress. Start capture continues after your latest saved sheet.

For f, g, j, p, q and y, put the body on the baseline and any tail below it. These lowercase letters keep the drawn baseline; other lowercase letters settle onto it automatically. Capitals reach the tall-letter line. Capitals, numbers, punctuation and joined pairs keep their drawn baseline; write joined pairs as a connected shape. Review samples lets you adjust placement or exclude a sample without deleting it.

On symbol sheets, pale a letters show the context of the blue positioning example. Draw only the labelled symbol in your own handwriting. Symbol placement hides or shows these references and their hints; they are never part of your saved writing. Quotes sit high, commas dip below the baseline, and an underscore belongs just below it.

Practice is optional and is not saved. Open Guides to switch each line or shading on or off. For a baseline only, leave Baseline ticked and untick the other guides and Shading. Letter labels and box dividers stay visible. Open free writing. Pencil or mouse draws; fingers use controls. Drafts offers recovery of unfinished writing. Use Save sheet to keep it in your alphabet before backing up. Use Backup / Import to keep a copy of saved work.
