A4 calibration check

This procedure was physically checked and confirmed accurate on 8 October 2026.

  1. Place A4 portrait paper on the bed. Home the plotter at the paper’s top-left corner, with the pen raised.
  2. Run the pen-up shapes to check movement, then the pen down/up check at home.
  3. Draw the shapes, add dimensions, then mark the centre. Keep the same sheet and paper position for all stages.
  4. Measure the rectangle: 170 × 257 mm, with 20 mm margins. The circle is 160 mm across in both directions. The triangle is 150 × 210 mm. The 6 mm cross marks the centre, 105 mm from the left edge and 148.5 mm from the top edge.

Positive X is right; negative Y is down. Pen up is Z0.5; pen down is Z5. Drawing speed is 900 mm/min. This checks the calibration; it does not change the machine settings.

Download the selected stage, then send its G-code from the Mac connected to the plotter. This page does not move the plotter directly. The pen check has no drawing preview.
