Ready for Pencil

Writing focus enlarges the sheet. Show controls brings back the toolbar. Undo, Redo and Save stay at the bottom. More contains Drafts and Download SVG.

Write a short word or sentence at your usual writing size. Pencil draws; fingers can use the buttons. Scrolling, pinching and text selection are blocked across this writing screen; the Writer box remains editable when the Pencil is lifted.

Save page keeps your original strokes in this browser. Download SVG exports a drawing. Smoothing changes the display and SVG only. Saved pages reopens earlier writing; saving edits creates a new copy. These pages are separate from character samples used by Compose. Drafts offers recovery of unfinished writing.
