Export font for Word and LibreOffice

Make an installable OpenType (.otf) font from this Writer’s included characters. Preferred blends are used first, then preferred captures, then available originals or saved blends. Unaccepted suggestions and excluded samples are never used.

Your pen strokes become filled outlines with rounded ends. Oversized or misplaced punctuation is fitted to your lowercase letter height, preserving its shape. Thickness only affects this export; your captures remain unchanged. No pair ligatures or natural variation are included. Missing characters may appear in a fallback font in your word processor.

Safe joins are added between neighbouring letters using contextual alternates. Captured shapes and spacing stay intact. Punctuation stays separate; unsafe connections are left open. Your Uppercase cursive joins choices are respected. Word and LibreOffice must enable contextual alternates.
